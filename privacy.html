Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 1, 2026

Royal Goa Ride ("we", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information.

Information We Collect

  • Name, phone number, and email address (for booking purposes)
  • Driving License and ID document (for verification)
  • Location data (for vehicle delivery)
  • Usage data via Google Analytics (anonymised)

How We Use Your Information

  • To process and confirm your vehicle rental booking
  • To deliver the vehicle to your location
  • To send booking confirmations and updates via WhatsApp
  • To comply with legal obligations

Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal data to third parties. Data may be shared with Google Analytics (anonymised) for website improvement purposes only.

Data Retention

Booking data is retained for 12 months. ID documents are deleted within 30 days of rental completion.

Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. Contact us at info@royalgoaride.com.
