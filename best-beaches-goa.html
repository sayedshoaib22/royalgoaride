Drive to Goa’s Best Beaches

Goa’s beaches are easiest to explore with a rental car. Here is the best beach itinerary for comfortable coastal drives, short stays, and scenic parking.

North Goa Favorites

  • Baga Beach: Best for water sports, night clubs, and a lively beach scene.
  • Calangute Beach: The largest north Goa beach, ideal for shopping, dining, and easy parking.
  • Candolim Beach: Quieter than Baga, with premium resorts and wide shoreline parking.
  • Anjuna Beach: Famous for the flea market and relaxed bohemian vibe.

South Goa Highlights

  • Colva Beach: Comfortable for families and easy to access from Margao.
  • Benaulim Beach: Relaxed seaside dining and quiet roads, ideal for slow drives.
  • Palolem Beach: A scenic drive to Goa’s southern tip with calm waters and beach huts.
  • Agonda Beach: Best for peaceful sunsets and long coastal drives.

Best Beach Route by Car

For a great mix of beaches and convenience, start in North Goa at Baga, work south through Calangute and Candolim, then head to Colva and finish at Palolem. This route uses Goa’s main coastal highway and avoids small inland detours.

Parking Tips for Goa Beaches

  • North Goa: Use resort or beach lot parking in Baga, Calangute, and Candolim.
  • South Goa: Choose municipal lots at Colva and Benaulim; pay daily rates are usually low.
  • Off-season: April to September offers easier parking and lower rental demand.
  • Early arrival: Reach popular beaches by 9 AM for the best parking spots.
