Goa Road Trip: Best Places to Visit by Car

Goa is best explored at your own pace. This blog guide walks you through the most scenic and memorable places to visit by car, from coastal sunsets to heritage forts and lively markets.

Start Your Trip in North Goa

Begin in North Goa with Baga Beach, Calangute, and Candolim. These beaches are perfect for water sports, nightlife, and easy-to-find parking when you rent a car.

Take a Scenic Stop at Chapora Fort

Chapora Fort is a short drive from Baga. It offers a classic view of the river meeting the Arabian Sea and is a great photo stop before heading south.

Explore Old Goa and Panaji

  • Old Goa: Visit basilicas, heritage churches, and Portuguese architecture.
  • Panjim: Walk along the Mandovi riverfront and explore small cafes and local markets.

Drive South for Quiet Beaches

Head south to Palolem, Agonda, and Colva for calmer beaches, scenic coastal stops, and easy sunset drives in a comfortable rental car.

Market Stops & Local Culture

  • Anjuna Flea Market: Best for souvenirs and street food.
  • Mapusa Market: Traditional Goan shopping for spices and textiles.
  • Colva Market: A great stop for local seafood and beachwear.
