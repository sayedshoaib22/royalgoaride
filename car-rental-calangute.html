Parking Guide for Calangute

Parking in Calangute can be challenging, especially during peak tourist season (November-February) and weekends. Understanding parking options saves time and money:

  • Calangute Beach Parking: Main parking lot near beach entrance charges ₹30-50 for 4 hours. Street parking available but limited. Peak hours (10 AM-3 PM) are crowded.
  • Tito's Lane Area: Very narrow lanes with minimal parking. Use street parking (first come, first served) or park at nearby commercial areas and walk. Night parking is safer than day parking.
  • Hotel/Resort Parking: Most hotels provide complimentary valet parking. The best option for overnight stays and worry-free exploration.
  • Shopping Complex Parking: Paid lots at local shopping centers offer ₹20-30 for 2 hours. Safest option if you're leaving car unattended.
  • PCB Bypass Parking: Free parking available along PCB bypass but parking vigilance recommended. Better for quick stops.

Road Conditions & Driving Tips for Calangute

Understanding Calangute's road network and seasonal variations helps you drive safely and efficiently:

  • Main Roads (PCB Highway): Well-maintained highway connecting Calangute to Baga, Candolim, and Panjim. Usually smooth but congested during 10 AM-3 PM and 5-7 PM.
  • Beach Road: Scenic but narrow, with multiple speed breakers and parked auto-rickshaws. Speed limit is 40 km/h. Most accidents happen here due to speeding.
  • Tito's Lane & Internal Roads: Extremely narrow lanes (single-car width) with parked scooters and uneven surfaces. Drive slowly and stay alert. Not recommended at night if unfamiliar.
  • Monsoon (June-September): Roads get waterlogged in low-lying areas near PCB bypass. Exercise extra caution, reduce speed, and avoid driving in heavy rain.
  • Night Driving: Main roads are well-lit and safe. Internal lanes lack streetlights. Avoid exploring new areas at night; stick to main routes.
  • Weekend Traffic: Calangute sees 3x traffic increase on weekends. Best time to explore is weekdays or very early mornings (6-8 AM).
