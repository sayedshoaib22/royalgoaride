By renting a vehicle from Royal Goa Ride ("we", "us", "our"), you agree to the following terms and conditions.

1. Eligibility

You must hold a valid driving license appropriate for the vehicle category. Minimum age is 18 years. Foreign nationals must carry an International Driving Permit (IDP).

2. Rental Period

The minimum rental period is 1 day (24 hours). Rentals are calculated on a per-day basis from the time of delivery.

3. Security Deposit

A refundable security deposit of ₹3,000 (cars) or ₹1,500 (bikes) is collected at the time of vehicle delivery. The deposit is refunded within 48 hours of vehicle return, subject to inspection.

4. Fuel Policy

Vehicles are delivered with a set fuel level. You are responsible for returning the vehicle with the same or more fuel. Fuel costs are not included in the rental price.

5. Vehicle Usage

  • Vehicles are for use within Goa only unless prior written permission is obtained.
  • Sub-letting or transferring the vehicle to a third party is strictly prohibited.
  • Driving under the influence of alcohol or drugs is prohibited.
  • Off-road usage (except for Thar rentals) is not permitted.

6. Accidents & Damage

In case of an accident, you must inform us immediately and file a police report. You are liable for damages beyond normal wear and tear. Full damage costs will be assessed against the security deposit.

7. Cancellation Policy

Cancellations made 24 hours or more before the rental start time receive a full refund. Cancellations within 24 hours may be subject to a one-day rental charge.
