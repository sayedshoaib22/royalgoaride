Essential Driving Tips for Thar Adventures

  • Steep Descents: Use low gear, engine braking. Avoid constant brake application.
  • Water Crossings: Maximum water depth 600mm. Test depth before crossing. Maintain steady speed.
  • Sand/Beach Driving: Reduce tire pressure for better traction (ask us for pump). Avoid extreme ruts.
  • Hill Climbs: Maintain momentum. Avoid stopping mid-incline. Use low gear for steep grades.
  • Monsoon Routes: Many off-beat roads become impassable June-August. Plan carefully.
