Mopa International Airport serves Pernem, Mandrem, Arambol, and North Goa beach destinations. Dabolim Airport (GOI) – South Goa Gateway

Goa's main international airport, Dabolim, welcomes flights from Delhi, Mumbai, Bangalore, and international hubs. Located 30 km from South Goa beaches (Colva, Benaulim, Palolem) and 45 km from Margao business district, Dabolim serves diverse travelers. Pre-book your airport car and arrive at your South Goa destination with confidence and comfort.
