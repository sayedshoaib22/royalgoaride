Popular Bike Riding Routes in Goa

🏝️ North Goa Beach Loop (40 km)

Baga → Calangute → Anjuna → Vagator → Chapora. Perfect 2-3 hour ride with multiple beach stops. Best time: early morning.

🏰 Fort Aguada & Lighthouse Route (25 km)

Start from Calangute, ride to Fort Aguada, scenic cliff views, and vintage lighthouse. Great for sunset ride.

🌴 Old Goa Heritage Route (35 km)

Panaji → Ponda → Old Goa → Spice plantations. Explore historic temples and churches. Full day adventure.

🌊 Coastal Highway Ride (60 km)

Margao → Baga via NH17. Experience Goa's longest beach road. Best for experienced riders.

Tips for Safe Biking in Goa

  • ✓ Always wear the provided helmet
  • ✓ Drive on left side (India uses left-hand traffic)

  • ✓ Watch for potholes during monsoon
