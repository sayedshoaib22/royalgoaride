Goa Travel Routes

Road trip ideas for beaches, forts, and scenic stops

Whether you are planning a short airport transfer or a full-day road trip, the right self drive car makes Goa easier to explore.

Calangute to Baga

Perfect for beach hopping and nightlife, with easy parking and fast access to the main North Goa circuit.

Margao to Palolem

A great South Goa route for beaches, cafes, and relaxed roadside stops.

Panjim to Dona Paula

Ideal for couples and quick scenic drives with beautiful viewpoints and coastlines.

Old Goa Heritage Route

Explore churches, forts, and heritage landmarks at your own pace with a dependable self drive car.
