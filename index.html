Driving Tips

Driving Tips in Goa

First time driving in Goa? Here's what you need to know before you hit the road.

Speed Limits

Speed limit is 40 km/h in cities and 60 km/h on state highways. Goa has regular speed checks — always carry your licence and rental documents.

Avoid Peak Hours

Tourist season (November–February) sees heavy traffic near popular beaches between 10am–12pm and 5pm–7pm. Plan your drives early morning for a smoother experience.

Monsoon Driving

Roads can get slippery during heavy rains (June–August). Drive slowly, use headlights, and avoid flooded roads. Some interior roads may be inaccessible.

Use Google Maps

Google Maps works very well in Goa. Download offline maps for your travel area before starting — some remote beach roads have poor mobile signal.

Parking at Beaches

Popular beach areas have paid parking. Arrive early to get spots close to the shore. Never park blocking traffic — local wardens are active.

Fuel Stations

Keep your tank topped up when exploring South Goa's remote areas or heading to Dudhsagar. Fuel stations can be sparse on certain routes. Check before you start.

Carry Documents

Always carry your original Driving Licence, rental agreement, and vehicle papers in the car. Police checkpoints are common, especially on NH66 and during festival seasons.

Emergency Contact

Save our WhatsApp number before you start driving. We're available 24/7 for any roadside assistance, navigation help, or emergency support during your rental period.

Must-Visit Places

Tourist Attractions in Goa

Explore Goa's iconic landmarks and hidden gems — all easily accessible with a Royal Goa Ride rental car.

Basilica of Bom Jesus, Old Goa

A UNESCO World Heritage Site and one of the finest examples of Portuguese Baroque architecture in Asia. Houses the mortal remains of St. Francis Xavier.

35 km from Margao · Easy parking nearby

Dudhsagar Waterfalls

One of India's tallest and most spectacular waterfalls, located inside the Bhagwan Mahaveer Wildlife Sanctuary. A must-visit on any Goa adventure. Best visited October–March.

60 km from Margao · 4x4 vehicle recommended

Chapora Fort

Made famous by the Bollywood film 'Dil Chahta Hai', Chapora Fort offers stunning panoramic views over Vagator beach and the Arabian Sea. Especially spectacular at sunset.

48 km from Margao · Free entry

Palolem Beach

Often cited as one of India's most beautiful beaches, Palolem's gentle crescent bay with calm waters makes it perfect for swimming, kayaking, and evening beach dinners.

37 km from Margao · Paid parking available

Fontainhas, Panjim

Goa's Latin Quarter is a charming labyrinth of narrow lanes, colourful Portuguese houses, Catholic chapels, and bohemian cafes. Perfect for slow walks and photography.

28 km from Margao · Street parking

Spice Plantations, Ponda

An authentic Goa experience away from the beaches — explore working spice farms growing cardamom, pepper, turmeric, vanilla, and more. Usually includes a traditional Goan lunch.

20 km from Margao · Half-day trip
